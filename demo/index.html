QUBO transaction ordering — verifier-gated, with canonical fallback

A batch is encoded as a QUBO, a quantum-inspired SA + tabu solver proposes an order, and a deterministic verifier re-simulates the constant-product AMM, checks constraints and scores it. If the solver fails or loses, the canonical order (fee desc, then seeded hash) is used. Everything below is computed in your browser.
